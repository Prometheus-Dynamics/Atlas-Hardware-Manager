## Build

- The user does not want a manual "keep it / go back" control while a new version is being tried. A trial resolves by itself: when the board comes back on the new version and answers healthy, Atlas confirms it; if it doesn't come back or isn't healthy within the watch, the board falls back on its own and Atlas shows a small notice that stays ("Update failed, still on <version>") until dismissed. Choosing a version by hand lives on the device's Software tab.
- Following a trial boot to its confirm or fallback by events, across the reboot, without holding anything else up.
