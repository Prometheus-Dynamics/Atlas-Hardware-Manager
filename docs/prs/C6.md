## Build

- Atlas does NOT confirm a trial. A board on a robot updates and reboots with no tool attached, so the board confirms itself: Lemnos's slot driver confirms after a trial boot once the board is healthy, within a deadline, and otherwise the next boot falls back; it records the outcome (confirmed by what and when, or fell back and why). This PR follows the outcome: it watches the board come back, reads the recorded result, and reports either the new version as current or a notice that stays ("the update to X did not hold, running Y", with the reason the board recorded) until dismissed. There is no confirm call from Atlas in the normal flow, and no "keep it / go back" control. `confirm()` is reachable only as an advanced action, for a board whose file sets a manual gate.
- Following a trial boot to its confirm or fallback by events, across the reboot, without holding anything else up.

## The surface

- Lemnos's brief, section "I5f": `list` carries `trial` (null, or the slot, attempts left, each attempt, the last state and the reason) and `resolved` (null, or the slot, the outcome `confirmed` or `fell-back`, by `auto` or `call`, the caller, the reason and the time). While `trial` is set the device shows "update still settling" beside its name and its health does not turn red: power cycles during a trial are normal. `resolved` with `fell-back` is the notice that stays, with the board's own reason.
