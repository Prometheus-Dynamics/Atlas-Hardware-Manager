# B42: The board answers for its hostname by mDNS on lemnos0

**Group:** B. Board descriptions

**Needs:** B24, B41

On 2026-11-22 the PC reached Raze #2 only by its serial-hash-v1 address, 172.31.209.217 (a /29 computed from the
serial). Nothing on the PC can name the board. zero-3e-bench already answers by mDNS on lemnos0
(`bench-network/files/20-bench-mdns.network`, systemd-resolved). raze-base turned resolved off in B41.

## Build

- raze-base answers for `<hostname>.local` on lemnos0 (and only there). The hostname is unique per board: derived from
  the serial, the way bench-hostname does it, or Lemnos's identity. Say which, and why.
- Choose the responder and give the reason: systemd-resolved with `MulticastDNS=yes` on lemnos0 (B41 took resolved
  out), or an Orion or Lemnos feature. Report the size cost in bytes either way (the lean rule).
- `scripts/check-devices.sh` refuses a product whose lemnos0 network file asks for mDNS while the responder is off.

## Proof

The image's files; check-devices refuses a product with mDNS asked for and no responder; and on a board, `<name>.local`
resolves from the PC once the PC has its lease (the lease is the user's host setting).
