# B28: the U-Boot environment partition holds two copies

## What

- `uboot-env` (GPT type "U-Boot ENV", 3de21764-95bd-54bd-a5c3-4abe786f38a8) is 128 KiB, at 16 MiB, on the RK35xx
  family (it was 64 KiB) and now exists on the sun60i family too (it had none).
- U-Boot is configured for a redundant environment: `CONFIG_ENV_SIZE=0x8000`, `CONFIG_ENV_REDUNDANT=y`,
  `CONFIG_OF_CONTROL=y`, with the existing `CONFIG_PARTITION_TYPE_GUID=y` and `CONFIG_ENV_IS_IN_MMC=y`.
  The sun60i family gets its first U-Boot fragment with the same lines.

## Why

Lemnos's slot record on a U-Boot board lives in U-Boot's environment, written as two copies so a power cut during
a write leaves the other (Lemnos's `L2-partition-requirements.md`, section 4, from U-Boot v2026.10's `env/mmc.c`).
Our RK35xx fragment had ONE copy of 0x10000 in a 64 KiB partition: a second copy would have been refused
(`env/mmc.c:139`, "too small for ... two copies"). The partition is 128 KiB, not the 64 KiB two copies of 0x8000
need, because a partition table cannot change after a board's first flash and the config can (coordinator's
ruling, 2026-11-13). Cost: 64 KiB of the card.

Boards affected: ZERO 3E, ROCK 5C, ROCK 4D (all inherit the RK35xx fragment and table), and the Cubie A7A, A7S,
A7Z (sun60i). None has been flashed with a slot image.

## Not in this PR

- No PARTUUID is stated anywhere: U-Boot and Lemnos's writer both find the partition by its type GUID on the boot
  disk.
- No Lemnos board-file key (`uboot_env_size`): that arrives with the Lemnos L2 move.
- At that move the environment lines leave these hand-written fragments for the one `lemnos-ctl tree
  --uboot-config` generates. Each fragment says so above the lines.

## Checked

- `scripts/check-devices.sh` at the pinned Gaia: passed (3 families, 7 boards, 14 components, 8 products), every
  product plans.

## Not proven

- No U-Boot was built with either fragment. Whether Radxa's U-Boot tree for the A733 has each symbol under the same
  name is not checked (the sun60i fragment says so).
- Nothing was written to a card or booted. On sun60i the environment now takes the 16 MiB offset DockSeed's chain
  used for its first filesystem partition; the filesystem partitions follow it.
- No gate check holds that a U-Boot fragment line takes (the kernel has one, `scripts/check-kernel-fragments.sh`).
  Gaia holds these three lines in its own fragment-line check.
