## Build

- Device identity, links, states (running, recovery, boot ROM, removable disk, robot device) and one capability set every driver answers from.
- The driver trait and registry. No driver may depend on another driver: dev's board driver pulls in the robot driver, and the image server pulls in the Orion driver. dev's unused `DeviceChannel` and the 'guess an older agent' rule do not come over.

## Proof

Tests: a driver registers and answers capabilities; a cargo-metadata test fails if any crate under crates/drivers depends on another.
