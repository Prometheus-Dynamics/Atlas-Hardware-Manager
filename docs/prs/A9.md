## Build

- The simulated Orion node moves from `crates/drivers/mock` to `crates/testing/sim-node` (`atlas-sim-node`), so a driver can use it in its tests without one driver depending on another. The mock devices stay in `crates/drivers/mock`. The isolation test stays as strict as it is.
- The registry keys drivers by family, and the board driver serves every family: a driver can declare that it serves any family, the family then comes from the identity it reports, and a driver that names a family still wins for that family.

## Proof

Tests: the board-driver case registers beside family drivers and is asked for a link they don't claim; the isolation test still fails on a driver-to-driver edge.
