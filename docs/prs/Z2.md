# Z2: the ZERO 3E bench product as a U-Boot slot image (carries Z1, PR #136 closed into this one)

Status: DRAFT. Rebased on next d1adf855 (PADS, PINS, PATCH-LISTS). Lemnos and Orion are at the merged pair
(Lemnos 31e0f736, Orion 671d29d). Untested on board.

## What this PR contains
- **From Z1 (carried, #136):** the ZERO 3E board's USB gadget (`lemnos-board.toml`: identity from the device tree,
  gadget with the Raze's addressing, `security` off), the `lemnos0-addressing` shared component and its removal from
  `raze-base-files` (raze-base product.toml: the component list), the `bench-pc-key` and `bench-network` components
  (Ethernet DHCP, mDNS), `bench-hostname` (shared component, prefix from `bench-hostname-prefix`), the checker's
  pc-key hunk, `scripts/build-product.sh`, `scripts/check-config-only.sh`, `scripts/pc-key-strip.sh`,
  `scripts/check-lemnos0-address.py`, `scripts/check-usb-ifname.py`. Z1's `bench-watchdog` is dropped (see Open).
- **Z2's own:** `devices/products/zero-3e-bench` as a `slot-disk` image (`slot-boot` recipe pattern, initramfs
  `lemnos-slot-init`, Lemnos components as this product's copies at 31e0f736), board component `zero-3e-slot-board`,
  the ZERO 3E slot-form `lemnos-board.toml` (record `uboot`, SD as U-Boot mmc 1, `uboot_env_size = 0x10000`,
  watchdog `/watchdog@fe600000`), the rk35xx U-Boot fragment lines (`CONFIG_ENV_MMC_USE_DT=y`,
  `CONFIG_ENV_MMC_DEVICE_INDEX=1`), the PSTORE lines in the rk35xx linux fragment, the ramoops dtsi.
  The Z1 `kernel/` directory is removed; its pieces moved to the board and the family.
- **Deleted:**
  - `devices/products/zero-3e-bringup` (the bench product replaces it).
  - `devices/products/zero-3e-base` (sd-image, no lemnosd, no watchdog owner). Its `os.fragment` is folded into
    `zero-3e-bench/os.fragment` (the bench inherited it; the fold keeps every line, and drops the base's
    `BR2_PACKAGE_SYSTEMD_RESOLVED is not set` because the bench sets it on). Its `product.toml` and `gaia.lock` are gone.
  - `devices/components/radxa-zero-3e-extlinux` (used only by zero-3e-base and the bring-up product). Its one comment
    in `rk35xx/family.toml` now says it was deleted.
  - References: `git grep zero-3e-base` and `radxa-zero-3e-extlinux` outside docs/prs: only the two history comments above.
    No CI workflow, script or test named either product.
- **Self-test fixture:** `devices/tests/fixtures/bringup/` (generic, not a live product: `product.toml`, `os.fragment`,
  `components/`, copied from rock-4d-bringup at this PR). The bring-up rule self-test copies it to a scratch
  `products/example-bringup` and mutates that copy. rock-4d-bringup is untouched.
- **Not in this PR:** C1 (the Cubie A7S bench, `cubie-a7s-bench`) was merged into this branch by an earlier
  commit and is removed again here; it is C1's own PR.
