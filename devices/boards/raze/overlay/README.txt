PLACEHOLDER. Generated by Lemnos from the board file (components/raze-lemnos-board/files/board.toml.PLACEHOLDER),
format pending Lemnos Q2a. Nothing here is compiled, and nothing in the image references it yet.

The generator (`lemnos-ctl tree board.toml -o board-overlay.dts`) writes ONE overlay that enables:
  - the I2C adapters (BMM150, INA238, and the IMU's PIO bus is Lemnos's, not an overlay);
  - the fan's PWM pin and the pwm-fan node (Lemnos's own PWM driver serves the fan; full duty until a
    valid temperature is read; there is NO fan overlay of ours);
  - the CSI receiver the camera needs, rp1_pio, cam0_reg disabled;
  - the watchdog node (lemnosd feeds it; 15 s by default, from the board file);
  - the USB device controller (the gadget: one configuration, FunctionFS "orion-link" + NCM + one ACM).

The same generator writes two more pieces, also PLACEHOLDERS:
  - the boot-time loader fragment (pin states at boot: USB power on, fan duty) in config.txt's generated
    block (boot/raze-device.txt, the marked placeholder);
  - the modprobe.d line for lemnos.ko (owned= and pisp_be=), not kept in this PR.

Until the generator output exists, the image is NOT bootable into a working board: no I2C, no fan control,
no camera receiver, no gadget and no watchdog from the device tree. Proof: a G-series board run (G1).
