PLACEHOLDER: the Raze's device-tree overlay is GENERATED by Lemnos, not kept here.

Lemnos will generate one overlay from this board's Lemnos board file (components/raze-lemnos-board/
files/board.toml), enabling exactly what the board's devices use: the I2C adapters (BMM150, INA238),
the fan's PWM and its pwm-fan node, the CSI receiver the camera needs, rp1_pio, and the USB device
controller for the gadget. Nothing in this directory is compiled.

Pending from Lemnos: the generator, its output name, and how Gaia builds it (kernel.overlays, or a
generated file). Until it exists the board has NO fan control, NO I2C (BMM150 and INA238 are off),
NO camera receiver and NO USB gadget from the device tree. Provisional.

Loaded by config.txt beside the product's own overlays: vc4-kms-v3d-pi5 and ramoops-pi5 (see
config/raze-device.txt), which come from the Pi firmware.
